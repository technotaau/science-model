                          Viva practice — one question at a time

                          🎧 Judge mode: the website asks the question aloud, listens to your spoken answer, tells you what you covered, then asks a follow-up — like a real judge. Works in Chrome or Edge.

                          🎙️ Voice Coach

                          🎧 Listen, then repeat after me

                          Pick a line. Press ▶ Listen to hear how it sounds with pauses. Press 🔁 Repeat after me: the website says one part, then waits for you to say it back, and checks you.

                          ❓ Ask a question — hear the answer

                          Ask anything about your project, for example “How does a fuel cell work?” or “What goes in the black bin?”. The answer comes only from your own notes on this website, so it matches what you have prepared.

                          🎙️ Record yourself

                          Explain one part of the model. The website checks your volume, speed and filler words (um, uh, like…).

                              🙂 Expression tips

                                🗣️ Say the hard words clearly

                                Press 🔊 to hear the word slowly, then 🎤 to say it yourself. Capital letters in the hint show which part to stress.

                                🪞 Mirror practice (for Expression marks)

                                Watch yourself while you explain. Reminders will appear on the screen. You can also record a video and watch it later.

                                The camera works only on this device; nothing is uploaded.

                                  🧘 1-minute confidence warm-up

                                  Do this just before practice, and on fair day before your turn. Breathing slowly calms nervousness; the tongue twisters wake up your voice.
